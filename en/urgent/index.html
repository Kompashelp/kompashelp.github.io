Urgent

Detained right now?

Give no statement without a lawyer: under art. 51 of the Constitution you may refuse to testify against yourself. Keep your lawyer's and family's numbers on paper. Someone close can write for you: Net Freedoms or the OVD-Info bot will help find a lawyer.

If you feel very bad or are thinking about suicide

You are not alone, and this can be survived. If a life is in danger, call 112. Below are free lines where someone will listen. State lines in Russia are tied to your number: do not talk about your case or politics there.

Russia

  • +7 495 989-50-50Emergencies Ministry emergency psychological help State line. The call is linked to your number: don't talk about your case or politics.
  • 051Moscow psychological help service 051 (с городского в Москве), 8 495 051 (с мобильного). City government service.
  • 8 800 250-18-59Russian Red Cross psychological support Also works with military families.
  • 8 800 2000 122Children's helpline 8 800 2000 122 (с мобильного также 124). For children, teenagers and parents. Run by a state foundation.

EU

  • 112Emergency number Works in Russia, EU countries and Georgia. In Lithuania, 112 takes acute suicidal crises in Lithuanian, English, Russian and Polish (UNHCR).

  • 0800 111 0 111TelefonSeelsorge 0800 111 0 111, 0800 111 0 222, 116 123. German only (as their international help page says).
