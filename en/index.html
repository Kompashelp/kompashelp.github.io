Detained right now?

Give no statement without a lawyer: under art. 51 of the Constitution you may refuse to testify against yourself. Keep your lawyer's and family's numbers on paper. Someone close can write for you: Net Freedoms Project or the OVD-Info bot will help find a lawyer.
