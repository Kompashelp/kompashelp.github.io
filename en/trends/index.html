Who counts what

Each project has its own rules on whom to count, so the figures of different projects are never added up or compared directly. The projects count only the cases they know of, so the real number of people may be higher.

ProjectWhom it counts
Memorial Political Prisoners SupportPeople the project has recognised as political prisoners under PACE Resolution 1900 (2012): prosecuted solely for exercising civil rights, for their beliefs or for belonging to a group, or with serious breaches of the law for a political motive. People who used violence against a person or called for violence on grounds of race, religion or ethnicity are not counted. The figure is those currently held. The project's rules
OVD-Info (anti-war criminal cases)Anti-war criminal cases only, not all political ones. Counted are people deprived of liberty: in pre-trial detention, penal colonies or compulsory psychiatric treatment. All defendants in such cases are counted separately. The project revised its dataset in May 2025; earlier figures are not comparable. The project's rules
Crimean Tatar Resource Center (Crimea)Crimean residents the centre counts as political prisoners: the sum of two parts of its chart — serving a sentence in a penal colony and held in pre-trial detention (we added them up, within one source). The centre's full list covers political prisoners and people prosecuted in criminal cases during the occupation. The project's rules
Viasna Human Rights Centre (Belarus)People Viasna has recognised as political prisoners in Belarus. Besides them, human rights defenders know of more than 10,000 people charged in political criminal cases since 2020; Viasna says this is far from everyone who has been persecuted. The project's rules
