How many people are behind bars

  • 1,843 recognised political prisoners currently held; 2,508 people recognised as political prisoners since 2009 · Memorial Political Prisoners Support, as of 10 October 2026
  • 348 people deprived of liberty in anti-war criminal cases; 1,384 people charged in such cases in total · OVD-Info (anti-war criminal cases), as of 10 October 2026
  • 385 political prisoners from Crimea currently held in places of detention; 560 people on the centre's list in total · Crimean Tatar Resource Center (Crimea), as of 10 October 2026
  • 879 political prisoners · Viasna Human Rights Centre (Belarus), as of 30 September 2026
