Corrections

We record every error we find here: what was wrong and what we changed. Nothing is removed silently.

26 September 2026 · Guides 'Arrest of property and accounts of people who left' and 'I am on the Rosfinmonitoring list'

The texts said a court decides on arresting the property of people abroad 'within one working day' and that the arrest follows a fine; the article number was given as '20.34'; and that property deals for listed people are possible with Rosfinmonitoring's permission.

What we changed. Per the law: the arrest is possible once the case is opened and only for acts abroad, the court decides no later than the next day, other property is arrested if accounts do not cover it, the article is 20.3.4. For listed people, registration of property deals is suspended until removal from the list; the law has no permission procedure.

26 September 2026 · Guides 'Phone check' and 'Connectivity and VPN'; the Safety page

The texts said the rule on border phone checks was not public, that Telegram works intermittently in Russia and that an empty phone raises questions. Outdated: law 257-FZ has been in force since 23 July 2025 with a penalty under art. 18.7 of the Code of Administrative Offences for refusing to hand over a device; Telegram has been almost completely blocked since April 2026; an empty phone is better than your main one if there was no time to prepare an ordinary one.

What we changed. The texts were updated and sources added.

26 September 2026 · Help others → Donate; News

On the donation page Meduza, The Insider and iStories were missing the 'foreign agent' designation, and Mediazona was missing its Belarus mark. News items from Memorial and Vot Tak did not show their designations.

What we changed. Designations were reconciled across all sections. The site now will not build if one organisation has different designations in different places.

26 September 2026 · What happened → Human Constanta, Free Belarus Center, Sphere

The warning before the contact button said three organisations are designated 'extremist' in Russia. That was wrong. Human Constanta was designated an 'extremist formation' in Belarus in May 2026, and we showed an outdated status (materials only). For Free Belarus Center only its materials are designated 'extremist' in Belarus. Sphere itself is not designated; in Russia the 'LGBT movement' is.

What we changed. Human Constanta's status was updated. Each of the three organisations now has an accurate warning. Russian and Belarusian designations are checked separately.

26 September 2026 · Guides 'Donations' and 'Released'

The guides said a person on the Rosfinmonitoring list may spend no more than 10,000 roubles a month. That limit was abolished on 1 June 2025.

What we changed. They now say income may be spent within the minimum wage per month for the person and each dependent family member (27,093 roubles in 2026).

26 September 2026 · What happened → OVD-Info

The OVD-Info card listed the 8 800 hotline number. According to the organisation itself, the hotline has been switched off.

What we changed. The number was removed; the card says only the bot works. A warning about the organisation's status was added.

26 September 2026 · What happened, guides → RosUznik

RosUznik was listed as an active service for letters to political prisoners. The freshness audit found it has not been updated since June 2024 and still lists people who have been released.

What we changed. RosUznik was removed from the directory, the guides' tools and the letter-writing rules. Addresses can be found in the Vestochka directory.

26 September 2026 · What happened → Freedom Now

The link pointed to freedomnow.org, a domain that does not belong to the organisation and was up for sale; anyone could have bought it.

What we changed. The link now points to the official site freedom-now.org. All 72 contacts in the directory were re-checked against the organisations' own sites.
