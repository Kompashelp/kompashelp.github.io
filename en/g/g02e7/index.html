Prison transfer: a relative has been moved and gone silent
  • A transfer lasts from a few days to several months; people are usually held up to 20 days at each transit point. There is almost no contact during this time.
  • Before the transfer: send food, water, warm clothes, stamped envelopes, reply forms and a sturdy bag.
  • The colony must notify a relative within 10 days of the prisoner's arrival (art. 17 of the Penal Code). If you hear nothing, write to the SIZO they left from and to the prison service.
  • If the person has 'disappeared', complain to the prosecutor, the regional prison directorate, the monitoring commission and the ombudsman. A lawyer can file inquiries. Publicity sometimes helps, and sometimes the prisoner is punished for it. Decide together with the lawyer and, if possible, with the person themselves.
  • Where to turn: Prison Lawyer, Solidarity Zone, First Department.
