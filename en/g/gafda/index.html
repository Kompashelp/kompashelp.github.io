Complaints to the UN and ECHR after Russia left the Council of Europe
  • The ECHR only accepts complaints about violations before 16 September 2022. Russia doesn't currently comply with its judgments, but they put violations on record.
  • For later violations there's the UN Human Rights Committee: you must go through four instances (first, appeal, two cassations), the filing deadline is 5 years, and registration takes about a year.
  • In February 2026 the Supreme Court called the UN Committee's interim measures non-binding, but not its final decisions. Human rights groups say complaints are still worth filing.
  • It's best to prepare with a lawyer from the start: violations must be raised in Russian courts first, or the UN may not consider them.
  • Where to turn: Memorial, the Institute for Law and Public Policy, First Department.
