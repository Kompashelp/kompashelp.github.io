For relatives of political prisoners: letters, parcels, help

  • Addresses for letters are on Politzek.me ('extremist' in Belarus; from Belarus, open it on a device that is not checked). Human rights groups advise keeping letters neutral, with no politics or other people's names, or the censor won't pass them.
  • BYSOL, Dissidentby and Politzek.me help with parcels and legal fees (all 'extremist' in Belarus; see the risk in the next point); INeedHelpBY sends food packages to families.
  • Helping with parcels has itself led to prosecutions, so sending money through funds is safer than paying directly. But people in Belarus have also been prosecuted for receiving help from funds designated 'extremist'. Ask the fund itself how to receive money more safely.
  • After release: group therapy and support at the Belarusian House in Warsaw, resocialisation help from Dissidentby (both 'extremist' in Belarus).
  • A Belarusian colony must notify a relative of a prisoner's arrival within 3 working days, but only if the prisoner has made a written request (Belarus Penal Enforcement Code, art. 15). Some political prisoners are held for years with no contact with the outside world ('incommunicado'): in August 2026 Viasna named two people who have not been heard from for about three years.
Safety
  • Since April 2026 the KGB has designated more than twenty chats and pages of prisoners' relatives on Telegram, Viber and VKontakte as 'extremist formations'. Do not stay in such chats or post there from a device that may be checked: membership can be treated as taking part in a 'formation'. Get news about parcels and visits from the lawyer or directly from the institution.

Designations of organisations in this guide: Viasna Human Rights Centre (extremist in Belarus); BYSOL Belarusian Solidarity Foundation (extremist in Belarus); Dissidentby (extremist in Belarus); Politzek.me (extremist in Belarus); INeedHelpBY (extremist in Belarus); Belarusian House in Warsaw (extremist in Belarus). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.
