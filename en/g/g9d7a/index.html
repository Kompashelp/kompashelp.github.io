Your home is being searched
  • Ask to see the court order. In urgent cases a search can go ahead without one, with the court notified later (art. 165 pt 5 CPC).
  • The law requires two attesting witnesses (arts 170, 182 CPC). You can ask for neighbours to be called; any refusal should go into the record.
  • Call a lawyer straight away: you have the right to one, but the investigator does not have to wait for them to arrive.
  • By law you do not have to give passwords (art. 51 of the Constitution). In practice a refusal is sometimes met with pressure, so decide with your lawyer how to handle it. Turn on encryption and a passcode lock in advance. During a search, do not hide or switch off devices in front of officers: it can make your situation worse.
  • Human rights groups advise putting every violation into the comments on the record, asking for a full copy and watching that nothing is planted.
