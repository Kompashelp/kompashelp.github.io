Parcels and visits in SIZO and penal colonies
  • In SIZO, parcels are handed in at the window (with a passport) or via online services, up to 30 kg a month. Visits in SIZO need permission from the investigator or the court handling the case.
  • In a colony the number of parcels depends on the regime: general (6 parcels and 6 small packets a year), strict (4), special (3); no limit in a settlement colony. Medicines don't count.
  • Human rights groups advise not bringing your everyday phone to a visit and not wearing clothes with political symbols: devices can be seized and searched.
  • If an item is refused, you can challenge it with the facility head and the prosecutor. Medicines go through a separate procedure via the medical unit.
  • Where to turn: Prison Lawyer, Solidarity Zone; for women in prison and their families: Women's Term.
