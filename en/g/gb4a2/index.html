Summons: who sent it?

What to do depends on who is summoning you. Choose your case: below are the start of each guide and the links.

From the military enlistment office

Check your extract from the Summons Register. Call-up papers count as served in person, by registered post, in your Gosuslugi account, or 7 days after appearing in the Summons Register. Deleting your Gosuslugi account does not change this.

  • What happenedConscription and mobilisationwho helps and what to do →

From the police, Investigative Committee or FSB

First, check through a lawyer what kind of summons it is. Questioning is part of a criminal case: you may be summoned on paper, by phone or otherwise (art. 188 CPC). Ignoring a proper summons can mean being brought in by force.

  • GuideCalled in for a 'chat' or for questioningOpen →
  • What happenedDetained or summoned for questioningwho helps and what to do →

From a court

Find a defender in advance: under the Code of Administrative Offences it need not be an advocate; it can be someone you trust. Since June 2026 OVD-Info no longer arranges lawyers in Russia, so many people defend themselves.
