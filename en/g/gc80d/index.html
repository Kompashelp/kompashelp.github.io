Your computer: Windows and Mac

For anyone whose computer holds documents, chats or social media and could be seized in a search or inspected at a border.

The 5-minute minimum

  • Turn on disk encryption: Mac System Settings → Privacy & Security → FileVault; Windows 11 Settings → Privacy & security → Device encryption (BitLocker on Pro).
  • Don't keep the recovery key on the same computer. With a Microsoft account Windows stores the key in that account; decide whether you want it there.
  • Use a login password and auto-lock; shut down rather than sleep before travel or if you fear a search.
  • Keep the system and browser updated.

If you are at risk

  • Mac: Lockdown Mode if you may be targeted by spyware.
  • Windows Copilot+ PCs: make sure Recall (screen snapshots) is off.
  • Sign out of desktop messengers before travel; for trips to Russia or Belarus a laptop without work data is safer.

What not to do

  • Don't leave the computer on and unlocked unattended.
  • Don't install software from forwarded links or cracked versions.
  • Don't remotely wipe a seized phone or laptop (Find My, Find Hub) without talking to a lawyer first: it can be treated as destroying evidence.

What traces remain

  • Cloud sync keeps copies of files elsewhere; an encrypted disk is open while you are logged in.
