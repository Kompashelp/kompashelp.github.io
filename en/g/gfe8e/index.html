What you can be punished for online: a map of offences

The guides below are drafts: a lawyer has not reviewed them yet. This is not legal advice.

How to read this map

  • administrative vs criminal liability
  • 'administrative prejudice': a repeat after a fine becomes a criminal case; a person is 'considered punished' from the decision until a year after the fine is paid (CAO art. 4.6)
  • limitation periods and 'continuing' offences: for a post still online the clock starts when it is found
  • reference only, not legal advice

Sections of the map

How people are found

  • denunciations by neighbours, colleagues, followers
  • social media monitoring by Center E and the FSB
  • phone checks at the border and on arrest
  • Rosfinmonitoring and bank data
  • provocateurs in chats and fake 'Ukrainian handlers'
