Interpol, surveillance and abductions
  • Most Interpol Red Notices are not public. A full request about yourself and a complaint go to the Commission for the Control of Files (CCF). See 'Am I on a list?'.
  • Interpol's Constitution (art. 3) bars political cases. According to Interpol's internal data, about half of Russian files reviewed by the CCF did not comply with its rules.
  • CIS-channel searches bypass Interpol's filters. Abductions are known in Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Armenia, Georgia and Uzbekistan: people were lured to meetings or seized near bases.
  • Rights groups advise not agreeing to 'business' meetings and keeping threats as evidence for the police and a lawyer. Let one trusted person outside Russia know your route. Don't give details to family in Russia: they may be questioned.
  • Digital security: the Access Now helpline (24/7, Russian spoken) and First Department guides.
