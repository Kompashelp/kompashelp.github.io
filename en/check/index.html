Am I on a list?

Official registers and public databases where you can check whether you are listed, wanted or under restrictions. For each resource we note what it shows, whether you need to log in and whether it opens from abroad.

Read first
  • Not appearing in a database guarantees nothing: a case may have been opened recently, and databases are updated with a delay.
  • Looking at official registers is not prohibited, but state sites see your IP address and account login. Searching for or opening banned materials themselves can lead to a fine (art. 13.53 CAO).
  • Logging in to Gosuslugi leaves a record with your IP address and time on the state's side. Requesting a criminal record certificate or a summons register extract is a request the state can see.
  • Do not install the Ministry of Digital Development's certificate on your main phone. Use a separate device or browser for government sites.
  • There is no official service to 'check a travel ban'. Websites with that name are unofficial.

Registers and lists

Rosfinmonitoring list

Whether you are on the list of 'terrorists and extremists'. Being listed means frozen accounts and limits on transactions.

Usually does not open from abroad. Updates are posted by a third-party channel, t.me/bad_list_updates (not a government one).

Login: Not needed From abroad: often unavailable, needs a Russian IP
Open → fedsfm.ru

Foreign agents register (Ministry of Justice)

Entries for listed people and organisations, with an XLS download.

inoteka.io (see below) is easier to search, but it is an OVD-Info project.

Login: Not needed From abroad: opens
Open → minjust.gov.ru

Extremist organisations (Ministry of Justice)

The list of organisations banned as extremist.

Extremist materials list (Ministry of Justice)

Banned materials and the court rulings on them.

Viewing the list itself is safe. Searching for or opening the listed materials falls under art. 13.53 CAO.

Login: Not needed From abroad: opens
Open → minjust.gov.ru

Inoteka (unofficial aggregator)

Easy search across the foreign agent and undesirable organisation registers, with short profiles.

Unofficial. Created by OVD-Info, which is designated extremist in Russia: open it through a VPN and do not share it.

Login: Not needed From abroad: opens
Open → inoteka.io

Register of people abroad 'evading punishment' (284-FZ, Ministry of Justice)

Whether you are on the 284-FZ register: no consular services, notaries, Gosuslugi, property deals or powers of attorney.

The 284-FZ register is on the Ministry of Justice registers portal, in test mode since August 2026. The portal often does not open from abroad.

Login: Not needed From abroad: often unavailable, needs a Russian IP
Open → reestrs.minjust.gov.ru

Belarus: list of 'extremist formations' and individuals

Which organisations, chats and media are designated 'extremist formations'; a separate list of persons 'involved in extremist activity'.

Donating to or subscribing to a 'formation' can lead to criminal charges. Don't download the list files onto your everyday device.

Login: Not needed From abroad: often unavailable, needs a Russian IP
Open → mvd.gov.by

Belarus: KGB 'terrorist list'

Updates to the list of persons 'involved in terrorist activity': inclusion means frozen accounts and bans on transactions.

Official KGB channel; kgb.by usually doesn't open from abroad. Don't subscribe from your main account: the web preview is enough.

Login: Not needed From abroad: opens
Open → t.me

Interpol Red Notices

Public wanted notices.

Most notices are not public. A full request about yourself goes to Interpol's Commission for the Control of Files (CCF).

Login: Not needed From abroad: opens
Open → interpol.int

Interpol: Commission for the Control of Files (CCF)

How to ask whether Interpol holds data on you (including non-public notices) and how to challenge a Red Notice or diffusion.

Political cases are barred by Article 3 of Interpol's Constitution. Best prepared with a lawyer who knows CCF practice.

Login: Not needed; application form From abroad: opens
Open → interpol.int

Belarus: Interior Ministry wanted list

Open → mvd.gov.by

Last signs of activity: April 2025. Before writing, check on the organisation's own site that it still works.
