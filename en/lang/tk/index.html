«Kompas» — sizi ýa-da ýakynlaryňyzy yzarlasalar kömek

Bu terjimäni ene dilinde gürleýän adam barlamady. Doly we takyk maglumat — rus we iňlis dillerinde.

«Kompas» — maglumat kitaby: sizi ýa-da ýakynlaryňyzy tussag etseler, sorag etmäge çagyrsalar ýa-da sözleriňiz üçin yzarlasalar, näme etmeli we kime ýüz tutmaly. Saýt siz hakda hiç zat bilmeýär we maglumat ýygnamaýar.

Eger häzir tussag eden bolsalar

  1. Aklawçysyz hiç zada gol çekmäň.
  2. Aklawçy talap ediň we ýakynlaryňyza nirededigiňizi habar beriň.
  3. Adatça kanun özüňize garşy görkezme bermezlige mümkinçilik berýär. Muny nädip etmelidigini aklawçydan soraň.

Freedom for Eurasia

Investigations into corruption and human rights abuses in Central Asia, including the persecution of Tajik and other activists in Europe. Accepts confidential information.

A research and advocacy group, not legal aid for individual cases.

Other ways to reach them: freedomeurasia@protonmail.com

Turkmen Initiative for Human Rights (Chronicles of Turkmenistan)

Monitors human rights abuses in Turkmenistan. Accepts reports on persecution, exit bans, passports not renewed and pressure on relatives.

The old address chrono-tm.org no longer belongs to the organisation. Do not open it.

Other ways to reach them: hronikatm@gmail.com

Turkmen.news

Independent outlet covering Turkmenistan. Accepts reports of abuses, including pressure on Turkmen citizens abroad.

Independent media are blocked in Turkmenistan. Use a VPN.
