Need to leave: where to start
  • First check whether you can leave: a summons in the draft register, a bailiff debt over 30,000 roubles, a criminal case, or state-secret clearance. How to check: see 'Am I on a list?'.
  • On an internal passport you can enter Armenia (by air only), Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Belarus. Belarus is not a way out: since 2026 it shares an exit-ban database with Russia, and people have been detained at Minsk airport and handed to Russian officers. Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan have handed people over. Armenia does not extradite in political cases, but may detain a wanted person and keep them from leaving for months.
  • Before the border, prepare an ordinary phone with little personal data in advance. Border guards ask about the purpose of the trip, your job, relatives in Ukraine and your view of the war. Prepare short, truthful answers. Don't lie about things that are easy to check: tickets, work, relatives.
  • In advance: power of attorney for family, a second passport, apostilles on diplomas and civil-status documents, money not only on Russian cards. Much can't be done remotely later (see 'The register of people abroad').
  • Where to turn: Kovcheg, Go by the Forest, inTransit, Reforum Help; if a case against you may already be in the works: First Department.
  • If a case is being prepared against you. The law does not bar a witness from leaving. It does bar suspects and the accused (art. 15, Law 114-FZ). Leaving under an undertaking not to leave is not a separate crime, but it almost always leads to a wanted notice, arrest in absentia and a trial in absentia. Decide only with a lawyer: First Department advises people facing a case.
  • Children under 14 can no longer travel to Armenia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan or Belarus on a birth certificate. They need their own passport or an entry in a parent's 5-year passport (until the end of 2026). Check in advance whether the other parent's consent is needed.
  • Choose transit carefully. Turkey deported an anti-war Russian woman in July 2026, and Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan have handed people over. If you face a case, avoid changing planes in countries that extradite or deport people to Russia, and don't fly Russian airlines.
Safety
  • Let one trusted person outside Russia know your route.
  • Don't post where you are until you have arrived.
  • Answer questions at the border briefly and truthfully.
  • If you are taken aside for a 'talk' at the border, calmly ask whether you are detained. If so, ask for a written record and a lawyer, and invoke art. 51 of the Constitution.
  • Don't discuss your route on MAX, VK, WhatsApp or by phone. The fewer people know, the safer for you and for them.

Designations of organisations in this guide: Reforum Help (undesirable). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.

The Ark (Kovcheg)

Legal advice for people who have left (documents, residence permits, visas, asylum, extradition risk), temporary housing, free psychologists, mutual-aid chats by city.

Lawyers via the Telegram bots @kovcheglive_bot and @EmigrantHelpBot; psychologists and housing via forms on the website. The project was set up with support from the Anti-War Committee, designated 'terrorist' in Russia in March 2026; The Ark itself is a 'foreign agent'. Psychologists: 4-week online groups via a form on the website; waiting time from a few days to weeks.

Other ways to reach them: team@kovcheg.live
