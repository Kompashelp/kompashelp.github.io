Need psychological support
  • If you are in danger right now or thinking about suicide, call 112 or a crisis line. In Russia: the Emergencies Ministry's psychological help +7 495 989-50-50; in Moscow 051 (from a mobile 8 495 051); Russian Red Cross 8 800 250-18-59, 7:00 to 22:00 Moscow time; for children, teenagers and parents 8 800 2000 122. Numbers for other countries: see 'Psychologists and communities abroad'.
  • Anxiety, sleeplessness, guilt and burnout after a search, a loved one's arrest or years of activism are common reactions. Human rights groups advise not waiting until things get really bad. Projects for people facing persecution don't need the context explained.
  • Where to turn. First Line: psychological bot @PsyFirstlineBot, free and anonymous, one-off chat sessions, you can come back. Nora: two free sessions with a psychologist or psychiatrist for activists, volunteers, NGO and media staff, political prisoners and their families (apply via the website). After violence by officers: the Committee Against Torture Foundation (@bfkpp_help), a psychologist and a support group, anonymously if you wish.
  • Specific groups. LGBTQ+: Sphere, up to 4 free sessions (the project is a 'foreign agent'; the 'LGBT movement' is designated 'extremist' in Russia). North Caucasus: Marem and SK SOS (designated 'extremist') have psychologists for the people they help. Rory Peck Trust pays for therapy for freelance journalists. If you have left: see 'Psychologists and communities abroad'. Belarusians: see 'Released / deported'.
  • Psychologists and doctors. Russia has no law on psychological help and no psychologist confidentiality. By law, doctors, psychiatrists and psychologists can all be questioned as witnesses (art. 56 Criminal Procedure Code). A doctor discloses information without your consent at the request of an investigator, court or prosecutor (art. 13 of Law 323-FZ). In practice it is safer not to tell a specialist in Russia the details of your case or names.
  • Psychiatrists. A single voluntary consultation is not in itself being 'registered', but it stays in your medical records. Dispensary observation is imposed by a panel of doctors only for severe chronic disorders and can be appealed (arts. 26-27 of Law 3185-1). It brings restrictions on driving licences and weapons; since 2025 the police take part in monitoring some of these patients, and since March 2026 there is a federal register. Examination or hospitalisation without consent is allowed only for a severe disorder when a person is a danger to themselves or others, is helpless, or would seriously deteriorate without help; a court reviews hospitalisation (arts. 23, 29, 32-35).
  • Online sessions and documents. Russian online therapy platforms store data in Russia and, under their own rules, hand it over when security agencies request it (Yasno's policy says so, for example). Ask a psychologist in advance what they write down, where they keep it and when they may break confidentiality: for First Line, a risk of suicide or a threat to a child. If you are thinking about a complaint or asylum, ask for a written report with the date and the specialist's name and qualifications.
Safety
  • If your phone may be checked, turn on disappearing messages in the chat with your psychologist. Contact organisations with a designation from a different device.
  • A call to a state helpline is linked to your number. Don't talk there about your case, politics or links to organisations.
  • Don't keep reports or session notes on a phone that may be seized or checked at the border.

Designations of organisations in this guide: Sphere (foreign agent, "LGBT movement" designated extremist); North Caucasus SOS Crisis Group (foreign agent, extremist). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.

Whom to contact

5 organisations

First Line

Free, anonymous psychological support for people affected by, or afraid of, political persecution and for their families; an information line and safety guides.

Psychological help is in a separate bot, @PsyFirstlineBot: free, anonymous, one-off chat sessions with trained volunteer psychologists, 18+, no diagnoses or medication. Confidentiality is broken if there is a risk of suicide or a threat to a child.

The only official bot is @FirstLineHelpBot. It never asks for money or SMS codes.

Other ways to reach them: website · psychology bot

Nora

Help for activists, volunteers, NGO and media staff, political prisoners and their families: two free sessions with a psychologist or psychiatrist (sometimes more) on application via the website, a peer-support bot run by volunteers, spaces in Moscow and St Petersburg.

They reply within 10 days by email, Signal or Telegram. Choose Signal for the reply. The bot @nora_psyhelp_bot offers peer support, not a psychologist.

The only official bot is @nora_psyhelp_bot. It never asks for money or SMS codes.

Other ways to reach them: noraforpeople@gmail.com · website

Committee Against Torture Foundation

Free help for people who suffered torture or ill-treatment by police, prison staff, the National Guard and other officials: a psychologist and a support group, payment for medicines and tests, documents for court, lawyers.

A charitable foundation registered in 2019. Its website says it is not a 'foreign agent'. It helps people at liberty and in custody, their families and witnesses. You can write briefly and anonymously, without your real name.

The only official contact is @bfkpp_help; the foundation's website links to it.

The Ark (Kovcheg)

Legal advice for people who have left (documents, residence permits, visas, asylum, extradition risk), temporary housing, free psychologists, mutual-aid chats by city.

Lawyers via the Telegram bots @kovcheglive_bot and @EmigrantHelpBot; psychologists and housing via forms on the website. The project was set up with support from the Anti-War Committee, designated 'terrorist' in Russia in March 2026; The Ark itself is a 'foreign agent'. Psychologists: 4-week online groups via a form on the website; waiting time from a few days to weeks.

Other ways to reach them: team@kovcheg.live

Sphere

Legal, psychological (up to 4 free sessions with a psychologist) and emergency help for LGBTQ+ people from Russia.

The 'LGBT movement' is designated 'extremist' in Russia. Contact Sphere from a device that is not checked.

The only official bot is @spherehelpbot. It never asks for money or SMS codes.
