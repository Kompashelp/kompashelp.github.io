You are abroad and wanted in Russia
  • How to check: a criminal record certificate via Gosuslugi or a trusted proxy (the authorities see the request; do not go to a consulate in person), the Interior Ministry 'wanted' database, Interpol's public Red Notices (see 'Am I on a list?'). CIS-channel searches bypass Interpol's filters.
  • After a detention on an extradition request Russia usually has about 40 days to send papers. Apply for asylum at once, but with a lawyer: mistakes in the first application are hard to fix. A lawyer can also complain to the UN Human Rights Committee: its interim measures halted Movlaev's extradition from Kazakhstan.
  • High risk, including in transit: Kazakhstan (Yemelyanova was detained changing planes in Almaty), Kyrgyzstan (Russian Interior Ministry office in Bishkek since 17 Aug 2026), Uzbekistan, UAE, Turkey.
  • In 2026 Georgia resumed extraditions to Russia in criminal cases; a court cleared the extradition of Timofeev, a Furgal aide, and the minister has the final say. There have been no extraditions in political cases so far. Some people are refused entry, more often those born in Crimea, Abkhazia, South Ossetia or Donbas. Armenia does not extradite in political cases, but people were removed via the Gyumri base.
  • Germany has effectively closed humanitarian visas for Russians and BAMF rejects deserters en masse. Rights groups advise applying for asylum with a lawyer and collecting evidence of persecution in advance.
  • Where to turn: The Ark (Kovcheg), First Department, inTransit. If you are already in Kazakhstan, contact the Kazakhstan Bureau. The Anti-War Committee's 'Consuls' help if you face deportation. The Anti-War Committee is designated 'terrorist' in Russia. If you have ties to Russia, contacting it can become grounds for a case under 'terrorism' articles. Write to the 'Consuls' only if deportation is a real threat, and after talking to a lawyer.
  • How people end up wanted after leaving. If you left as a suspect or accused, the investigator declares you wanted, a court may order your arrest in absentia and try you in absentia. Russia usually adds a CIS-wide search right after the federal one, and under the Minsk Convention you can be detained even before an extradition request arrives.
  • The 284-FZ register is not immediate: it requires a final conviction or a 'political' administrative penalty and confirmed 'evasion'. Until there is a verdict, you have time to sort out documents and powers of attorney through a local notary.
Safety
  • Don't agree to 'business' meetings and stay away from Russian consulates and military bases.
  • Check your accounts for leaks and turn on two-step verification.
  • Choose routes with no changes in countries that hand people over to Russia.
  • Don't go back, even in transit via Belarus: it shares an exit-ban database with Russia and has handed people over at Minsk airport.

Designations of organisations in this guide: Anti-War Committee 'Consuls' (terrorist). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.

Kazakhstan International Bureau for Human Rights and Rule of Law

Protects Russians in Kazakhstan from extradition and deportation and helps with asylum; also takes cases of Kazakh activists and journalists.

Helps people already in Kazakhstan; lawyer Artur Alkhastov advises Russians. Entering or transiting Kazakhstan is risky: since January 2026 the authorities have extradited and deported political refugees.

Georgian Young Lawyers' Association (GYLA)

One of Georgia's oldest human rights groups: strategic litigation, defence of people detained at protests, ECHR applications.

The Ark (Kovcheg)

Legal advice for people who have left (documents, residence permits, visas, asylum, extradition risk), temporary housing, free psychologists, mutual-aid chats by city.

Lawyers via the Telegram bots @kovcheglive_bot and @EmigrantHelpBot; psychologists and housing via forms on the website. The project was set up with support from the Anti-War Committee, designated 'terrorist' in Russia in March 2026; The Ark itself is a 'foreign agent'. Psychologists: 4-week online groups via a form on the website; waiting time from a few days to weeks.

Other ways to reach them: team@kovcheg.live

Anti-War Committee 'Consuls'

Emergency help for anti-war Russians abroad facing deportation to Russia or refusal of residence or asylum: appeals to authorities, finding local lawyers. 13 EU countries, the UK and the US.

On 2 March 2026 Russia's Supreme Court designated the Anti-War Committee a 'terrorist organisation'. Contacting, taking part or donating can lead to charges under terrorism articles for anyone with ties to Russia or plans to travel there. They do not help people inside Russia. Use only if you face a real deportation threat.
