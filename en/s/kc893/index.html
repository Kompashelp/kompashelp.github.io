Draft What human rights defenders advise

Russian or Belarusian in Armenia
  • You can stay up to 180 days a year without registering; an internal passport works only on a direct flight from Russia. Law firms report that from 1 Nov 2026 longer stays need a paid certificate via permit.am; check.
  • The authorities publicly promise not to extradite in political cases. People are detained on Russian warrants. So far everyone we know of who was detained on a Russian warrant has been released, but that is no guarantee.
  • The main risk is Russian structures rather than the Armenian state: deserters were taken away via the base and consulate in Gyumri. Rights groups advise keeping away from them and refusing 'meetings about your case'.
  • If detained, tell your family, The Ark (Kovcheg) and HCA Vanadzor at once. Quick publicity has helped before, but decide on it together with a lawyer.

current as of 25 September 2026. More: The Moscow Times: Armenia pledges no political extraditions (undesirable); Meduza: abductions in Armenia, Georgia and Uzbekistan (undesirable); Vardanyan & Partners: changes from November 2026

Whom to contact

3 organisations

Helsinki Citizens' Assembly, Vanadzor (HCA Vanadzor)

Free legal aid to victims of rights abuses, including torture; ECHR cases. Documented the removal of a Russian deserter via the Gyumri base.

The hcav.am website may block automated access. Reach them via Facebook.

Other ways to reach them: hcav.am

Protection of Rights Without Borders (PRWB)

Armenian NGO: legal aid and court representation, monitoring of freedom of assembly and justice.

Not specialised in foreigners; ask whether they will take your case.

Other ways to reach them: info@prwb.am · +374 10 505303 (a call stays in the phone log and with the operator)

Armenia (Yerevan) contact matched to the organisation's site 26 September 2026
Get in touch → prwb.am

The Ark (Kovcheg)

Legal advice for people who have left (documents, residence permits, visas, asylum, extradition risk), temporary housing, free psychologists, mutual-aid chats by city.

Lawyers via the Telegram bots @kovcheglive_bot and @EmigrantHelpBot; psychologists and housing via forms on the website. The project was set up with support from the Anti-War Committee, designated 'terrorist' in Russia in March 2026; The Ark itself is a 'foreign agent'. Psychologists: 4-week online groups via a form on the website; waiting time from a few days to weeks.

Other ways to reach them: team@kovcheg.live
