Draft What human rights defenders advise

Police came to the family, or a relative has disappeared
  • Now: ask through the door for ID, which agency they're from and why they've come, and call a lawyer. Art. 51 of the Constitution lets you refuse to testify against yourself, your spouse and close relatives.
  • You can decline a 'chat' or an informal interview: it isn't an interrogation. Interrogations require a formal summons; human rights groups advise asking for it in writing and going with a lawyer.
  • If a relative was taken after a search: they may be at a police station (up to 48 hours), a temporary holding facility, then in SIZO by court order. Check district court websites, call duty desks, bring in a lawyer and the prison monitoring commission (ONK).
  • If you can't find them: send written inquiries to holding facilities and SIZOs in the region and neighbouring ones via FSIN-pismo or Zonatelecom. The case may be run from another city, such as Moscow.
  • Shared chats: human rights groups advise leaving sensitive group chats and not keeping other people's devices or papers at home. Whether to delete specific messages: ask a lawyer.
Safety
  • If you can, call a lawyer before you open the door.
  • Don't keep other people's devices or documents at home.
  • Decide only with a lawyer whether to delete messages with your relative.

Whom to contact

4 organisations

Net Freedoms Project

Legal hotline: cases over posts, reposts and comments, website blocking, detentions via facial recognition.

Set up by lawyers from the Agora human rights group, which Russia designated 'undesirable' in 2023.

The only official bot is @nfp_hotline. It never asks for money or SMS codes.

Other ways to reach them: Delta Chat

Prison Lawyer

Answers questions about imprisonment: rights in pre-trial detention and penal colonies, parcels, visits, parole.

The only official bot is @prison_hot_line. It never asks for money or SMS codes.

Nora

Help for activists, volunteers, NGO and media staff, political prisoners and their families: two free sessions with a psychologist or psychiatrist (sometimes more) on application via the website, a peer-support bot run by volunteers, spaces in Moscow and St Petersburg.

They reply within 10 days by email, Signal or Telegram. Choose Signal for the reply. The bot @nora_psyhelp_bot offers peer support, not a psychologist.

The only official bot is @nora_psyhelp_bot. It never asks for money or SMS codes.

Other ways to reach them: noraforpeople@gmail.com · website
