Read first

If you are in Belarus or carry a device that may be checked: do not open or subscribe to these organisations' sites and channels: it can be used to open a case. Save the contacts in advance and open them from a device you will not take across the border and that is not checked.

The guides below are drafts: a lawyer has not reviewed them yet. This is not legal advice.

Right now

  1. Remember: Belarusian consulates don't issue or replace passports; that happens only in Belarus.
  2. Ask about a Polish 'foreigner's travel document' if you have a residence permit.
  3. Note: states don't yet recognise the 'New Belarus passport' as a travel document.

Passport abroad: what to do
  • Since 7 September 2023 (Decree No. 278) consulates no longer issue or replace passports or certify powers of attorney for property deals. Only in Belarus.
  • In Poland, Belarusians holding a humanitarian visa (D 21) get a residence permit for 3 years at once, with no fee and no separate work permit.
  • With that permit you can get a Polish 'travel document for a foreigner'. The Belarus-specific concessions ran until 30.06.2026; we have not yet checked whether they were extended. Ask the voivodeship office before applying.
  • Pashpart.org has a combined guide to documents and safe travel. The 'New Belarus passport' is not yet recognised by states as a travel document.

Designations of organisations in this guide: Pashpart.org (Tsikhanouskaya's Office) (extremist in Belarus). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.
