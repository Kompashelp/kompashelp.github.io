Right now

  1. Hand in a parcel at the SIZO window with your passport, or online.
  2. Don't write about politics, the case or third parties: censors read letters.
  3. Write to the SIZO they left and the prison service if you hear nothing.

Draft What human rights defenders advise

Someone in prison is being denied treatment
  • Human rights groups advise putting requests for medical care in writing to the head of the medical unit and pressing for a copy of the medical record: without documents, violations are hard to prove.
  • If there is still no treatment, complaints go to the regional prison service, the prosecutor who oversees prisons and the health watchdog Roszdravnadzor. Publicity sometimes helps, and sometimes the prisoner is punished for it. Decide together with the lawyer and, if possible, with the person themselves.
  • A seriously ill prisoner can be released under art. 81 CC after a medical examination against the list of illnesses in Government Decree No. 54. A lawyer usually prepares the application.
  • In urgent cases lawyers ask the UN Human Rights Committee for interim measures.
  • Where to turn: Prison Lawyer, Public Verdict. Political prisoner support groups help with medicines and parcels.
  • In pre-trial detention. The list of illnesses that bar detention is set by Government resolution 3, updated in 2025. The defendant, their representative or lawyer applies to the investigator, court or detention centre head with medical records from inpatient tests. The decision on a medical examination is due the next working day; a refusal can be appealed.
  • A prisoner can attach their own medical records to an application for release on health grounds (resolution 54 as amended in 2025). OVD-Info reports that in 2025 prosecutors overturned such releases on appeal at least twice.
  • OVD-Info counted at least 225 political prisoners with health problems in 2025 and documented 11 cases of denied medical care. Copies of medical records are issued on the patient's own written request, usually within 30 days (Law 323-FZ, art. 22; order 789n).
Safety
  • Keep copies of medical records and complaints with the lawyer and family: without them violations are hard to prove.

Help for activists, volunteers, NGO and media staff, political prisoners and their families: two free sessions with a psychologist or psychiatrist (sometimes more) on application via the website, a peer-support bot run by volunteers, spaces in Moscow and St Petersburg.

Committee Against Torture Foundation

Free help for people who suffered torture or ill-treatment by police, prison staff, the National Guard and other officials: a psychologist and a support group, payment for medicines and tests, documents for court, lawyers.

A charitable foundation registered in 2019. Its website says it is not a 'foreign agent'. It helps people at liberty and in custody, their families and witnesses. You can write briefly and anonymously, without your real name.

The only official contact is @bfkpp_help; the foundation's website links to it.
