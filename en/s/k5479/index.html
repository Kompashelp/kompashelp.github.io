Persecuted in Kyrgyzstan
  • Kloop, Temirov Live and Ait Ait Dese are designated 'extremist': sharing or keeping their material can lead to charges (up to 3 years). Officials publicly call posts about China, land and foreign debt 'fakes'.
  • Bir Duino still works in the country (free legal aid, torture victim support, Bishkek and Osh). Human rights defenders: Front Line Defenders and ProtectDefenders.eu.
  • UN findings matter: the Supreme Court ordered a review of journalist Makhabat Tajibek kyzy's case after the UN Working Group found her detention unlawful.
  • Rallies in central Bishkek have been banned since 2022; people are also detained over social media 'calls'. Think about digital security before posting criticism.

current as of 25 September 2026. More: IPHR: Kyrgyzstan, August 2026; CPJ: Kloop and Temirov Live declared 'extremist'

Bir Duino Kyrgyzstan Human Rights Movement

Free legal aid, support for victims of torture and ill-treatment, monitoring of political cases. Offices in Bishkek and Osh.

Kyrgyzstan's 2024 'foreign representatives' law puts pressure on foreign-funded NGOs. Ask which programmes are currently running.

Other ways to reach them: Bishkek: +996 501 156-390 · Osh: +996 555 161-300 · birduinokyrgyzstan@gmail.com (a call stays in the phone log and with the operator)
