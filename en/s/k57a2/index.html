Draft What human rights defenders advise

Pressure on a journalist
  • Main risks: working with 'undesirable' outlets (284.1 CC, up to 4 years), breaking the foreign-agent law (330.1 CC), army 'fakes' (207.3 CC), unlawfully obtaining state secrets (283.1 CC).
  • Warning signs: summons to 'preventive talks', mentions in pro-government channels, surveillance, repeated border checks. Human rights groups advise consulting a lawyer straight away.
  • Keep work and personal devices separate, don't keep conversations with sources in mainstream messengers, and send footage only once you're somewhere safe.
  • If you've been designated a foreign agent or summoned for questioning, see the notes on those situations.
  • Where to turn: Mass Media Defence Centre, First Department; if forced to leave: Reforum Help. Belarusian journalists: BAJ.

Designations of organisations in this guide: Reforum Help (undesirable). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.

current as of 25 September 2026. More: First Department: guide for journalists in Russia (foreign agent); First Department: why a journalist needs a press card (foreign agent)

Reforum Help

Free psychologists and lawyers for forced emigrants: migration and visas, taxes, civil law, humanitarian residence; groups and seminars. Psychologists work in Russian and Belarusian.

A Reforum project of the Free Russia Foundation, designated 'undesirable' in Russia. If you travel to Russia, contacting an 'undesirable' organisation or joining its events can lead to prosecution, so check with a lawyer.
