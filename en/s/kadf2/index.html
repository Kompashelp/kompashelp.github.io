Right now

  1. Check your entry in the summons register: every sign-in there is logged.
  2. Don't go to the enlistment office because of a phone call.
  3. Collect papers for a deferral or alternative civilian service in advance.

If someone close is being drafted: the call-up decision can be appealed, including online.

Draft What human rights defenders advise

Call-up papers from the military enlistment office
  • Call-up papers count as served in person, by registered post, in your Gosuslugi account, or 7 days after appearing in the Summons Register. Deleting your Gosuslugi account does not change this.
  • A ban on leaving Russia applies from the moment the summons is in the register. 20 days after a missed appearance date: a driving ban, no car or property deals, and limits on loans and self-employment.
  • Since 1 January 2026 conscription runs all year: summonses and medical boards in any month; dispatch to units 15 Apr-15 Jul and 1 Oct-31 Dec.
  • Human rights groups usually advise not going to the enlistment office because of a phone call, checking your register entry and preparing papers for a deferral or alternative civilian service in advance.
  • Where to turn: Go by the Forest (Idite Lesom), only the @iditelesom_info_bot bot (there are clones); the Conscientious Objectors Movement; Call to Conscience; Soldiers' Mothers of St Petersburg; Conscript School (a 'foreign agent').
Safety
  • Gosuslugi and the draft register log every time you sign in.
  • Don't write in chats where you live or where you are going.
  • Save Call to Conscience's contacts outside Telegram too.

Whom to contact

4 organisations

Call to Conscience

Helps people refuse military service: advice for conscripts, mobilised soldiers, contract soldiers and their families, court defence, conscientious objection.

The only official bot is @agsnowarbot. It never asks for money or SMS codes.

Other ways to reach them: Delta Chat · agsnowar@gmail.com · website

Go by the Forest

How to avoid conscription and mobilisation; helps servicemen leave the country and find asylum.

The only official bot is @iditelesom_info_bot, 10:00–22:00 Moscow time. The project never writes first and never takes money.
