Draft What human rights defenders advise

LGBTQ+ in Russia after the 'movement' was declared extremist
  • Human rights groups advise removing rainbow symbols from public profiles, making accounts private, leaving VK and not reposting initiatives' material. Don't send money to LGBT initiatives from Russia from any card: it can be treated as financing extremism.
  • What is punished: public display of symbols (a fine or up to 15 days' arrest, art. 20.3 CAO); 'participation in an extremist organisation' (up to 6 years, art. 282.2 CC); organising and financing (more).
  • Be careful with dates: set-ups via apps and raids on clubs are known. Before a party, keep a lawyer's number on paper and agree who to alert if you're detained.
  • If detained: invoke art. 51 of the Constitution, ask for a lawyer, don't unlock your phone. In the North Caucasus, if threatened by family or police, contact NC SOS. NC SOS was designated 'extremist' in Russia in 2026, but the group says asking it for help does not count as taking part in its activities. Write from a device that is not checked.
  • Where to turn: Sphere (foreign agent), SK SOS ('extremist' in Russia); abroad: Quarteera ('undesirable' in Russia). Contact them from a device that is not checked. Psychological support: Sphere, First Line.
Safety
  • Meet people from dating apps only in busy places, and tell a friend.
  • Don't send money to LGBT initiatives from any card.
  • Keep a lawyer's number on paper.

Designations of organisations in this guide: Sphere (foreign agent, "LGBT movement" designated extremist); North Caucasus SOS Crisis Group (foreign agent, extremist); Quarteera (undesirable). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.

Equality Movement

The largest LGBTQ+ community organisation in the South Caucasus: lawyer, psychologist, social worker, doctor, HIV testing.

Georgia has had an anti-'LGBT propaganda' law since 2024. The latest publication on the site is from May 2026 (annual report); check that services are running.

Quarteera

Association of Russian-speaking LGBTQ+ people in Germany: advice for refugees, community and support.

Designated 'undesirable' in Russia. If you travel to Russia, contacting it or joining its events can lead to prosecution, so check with a lawyer.

Sphere

Legal, psychological (up to 4 free sessions with a psychologist) and emergency help for LGBTQ+ people from Russia.

The 'LGBT movement' is designated 'extremist' in Russia. Contact Sphere from a device that is not checked.

The only official bot is @spherehelpbot. It never asks for money or SMS codes.

North Caucasus SOS Crisis Group

Evacuates LGBTQ+ people, their loved ones and women threatened by their families from Chechnya, Ingushetia and Dagestan; shelter, doctors, psychologists, lawyers.

Designated 'extremist' in Russia in summer 2026. The group says asking it for help does not count as taking part in its activities. It no longer has staff or volunteers inside Russia.

The only official bot is @sksos_helpbot. It never asks for money or SMS codes.
