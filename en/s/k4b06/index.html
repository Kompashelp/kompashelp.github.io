Draft What human rights defenders advise

Journalist or blogger in Central Asia
  • Journalists face charges of 'false information', 'calls to riots' and 'inciting discord'; Kyrgyzstan has designated Kloop and Temirov Live 'extremist', and seven journalists are jailed in Tajikistan.
  • Get legal help early: in Kazakhstan, Adil Soz; elsewhere, CPJ and RSF (note RSF is 'undesirable' in Russia).
  • In Uzbekistan criticism is taken down via fake copyright complaints and people are summoned over likes. Keep an archive off-platform and secure your accounts (Access Now).
  • If you are in exile, do not go home for documents and expect pressure on relatives (see the situation on persecution abroad).

Designations of organisations in this guide: Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) (undesirable). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.

current as of 25 September 2026. More: CPJ: Kloop and Temirov Live declared 'extremist'; IPHR: new risks for civil society in Uzbekistan; IPHR: pressure on media and the 'LGBT propaganda' law
