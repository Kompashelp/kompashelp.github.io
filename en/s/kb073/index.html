Read first

If you are in Belarus or carry a device that may be checked: do not open or subscribe to these organisations' sites and channels: it can be used to open a case. Save the contacts in advance and open them from a device you will not take across the border and that is not checked.

The guides below are drafts: a lawyer has not reviewed them yet. This is not legal advice.

Right now

  1. Report the detention to Viasna and BYSOL; from Belarus, via a trusted person abroad.
  2. Find a lawyer, and contact rights groups only from devices that aren't checked.
  3. Arrange notarised guardianship papers if there are children in the family.

Draft What human rights defenders advise

Someone close has been detained in Belarus
  • Human rights groups advise reporting the detention to Viasna (@ViasnaSOS) and BYSOL and finding a lawyer. Both are designated 'extremist' in Belarus, and contact with them can lead to prosecution. If you are in Belarus, pass on information through a trusted person abroad, or from a device that is not checked, over a secure channel.
  • Detainees are often pushed into confessing and recording 'repentance' videos, which are later used in the case. It helps if they know they may refuse to testify against themselves.
  • Pressure extends to the family, up to declaring it 'socially dangerous' and threatening to remove children. Arrange notarised guardianship papers in advance.
