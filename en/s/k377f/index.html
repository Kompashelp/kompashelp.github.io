Draft What human rights defenders advise

Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan: pressure on family and persecution abroad
  • Deportation home happens even from the EU and Turkey: since 2023 Germany has deported four Tajik activists, all arrested on arrival. Apply for asylum with a lawyer and document threats.
  • Do not send your passport to a consulate for renewal or go home for documents: Turkmen passports are not renewed and exit bans follow. Find out about refugee travel documents in advance.
  • If relatives are summoned or forced to disown you, note dates and names. Pass on or publish information about relatives only with their consent and after talking to rights groups: HRW, Freedom for Eurasia, Uzbek Forum, Chronicles of Turkmenistan.
  • Russia, the UAE and Turkey are not safe: Pamiri, Karakalpak and Turkmen activists have been returned from there, including on Interpol notices.

current as of 25 September 2026. More: HRW: Tajik activist deported from Germany; HRW: Turkmen activists at home and in exile; HRW: World Report 2026, Uzbekistan
