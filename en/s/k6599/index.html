Donations, Telegram Stars and money
  • Telegram Stars and paid reactions are not anonymous: the channel owner sees the sender and the history stays in your account. People are already being charged with treason and financing terrorism over them.
  • There is no minimum amount: a case is possible over a single Star. In September 2026 a Tyumen man got 8 years for Stars sent to the channels of Michael Nacke, Alexander Nevzorov and Alexander Shpak.
  • Before donating, human rights groups advise checking the recipient's status (see 'Am I on a list?'). Donating to an 'extremist', 'undesirable' or 'terrorist' organisation can lead to a criminal case.
  • In Telegram settings you can hide yourself from the 'top senders' list. If you donated after an organisation was banned, talk to a lawyer about what to do with the account. If a case has already been opened, delete nothing: deleting can be used against you.
  • How donors are found. Almost every FBK donation case rests on a bank statement showing a transfer to one Stripe account. Rosfinmonitoring searches for payers centrally; cases are opened 3-4 years after the donation. Transfers from foreign cards come to light when the phone is checked at the border. If you have been put on the list, see 'I'm on the Rosfinmonitoring list'.
  • If a case is open, the donation date matters. The law in force at the time of the transfer applies (art. 9 CC); a harsher law is not retroactive (art. 54 of the Constitution). Per OVD-Info, FBK donations from 5 August 2021 to 11 February 2026 count as financing extremism (art. 282.3, up to 8 years). Only later ones may count as financing terrorism (art. 205.1, from 8 years).
  • How donors are found. According to Mediazona, almost every FBK donation case rests on a bank statement showing a transfer to one account at the payment service Stripe. Rosfinmonitoring searches for payers centrally; cases are opened 3-4 years after the donation. Phones are examined to prove the person knew about the ban.
  • What to expect. By April 2026 Mediazona counted at least 225 cases over FBK donations. Of 167 known verdicts, 114 were fines and 24 were prison terms. Terms of 10-15 years came when transfers to the Ukrainian army were added. Transfers to Ukrainian funds are tried as treason (art. 275, from 12 years).
Safety
  • Check the recipient's designation before donating.
  • Telegram 'stars' and transfers are visible to the recipient and the bank.
  • If you travel to Russia, don't pay designated organisations with a Russian card.
  • If a case is already open, delete nothing: no accounts, no banking apps. Talk to a lawyer first.
  • Do not write a 'confession' or give statements without a lawyer. The exemptions in the notes to arts. 282.3 and 205.1 require helping to stop a crime; we found no case where they were applied to donors.

Designations of organisations in this guide: OVD-Info (foreign agent, extremist, on Rosfinmonitoring list). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.
