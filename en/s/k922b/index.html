Messaging, VPNs and blocking in 2026
  • WhatsApp, Signal and Viber are blocked in Russia. Since April 2026 Telegram has been almost completely blocked in Russia and usually does not open without a VPN. Mobile internet gets shut down. Save your lawyer's and rights groups' contacts outside Telegram in advance: email, Delta Chat, phone.
  • By law using a VPN is not banned. In practice, since 1 September 2025 art. 13.53 CAO fines people for deliberately searching for material on the extremist list, including through a VPN. Don't forward links to VPNs or VPN bots in chats: this can be treated as advertising tools for bypassing blocks (art. 14.3 CAO), and people are fined for it.
  • Blocking comes in waves, so specialists advise keeping two or three VPNs of different types and updating them in advance, not when the shutdown hits.
  • The state messenger MAX has no end-to-end encryption, so don't discuss anything sensitive on it. A school cannot make you install MAX.
  • Detailed guides: First Line (digital security section) and EFF's Surveillance Self-Defense in Russian.

Whom to contact

4 organisations

Net Freedoms Project

Legal hotline: cases over posts, reposts and comments, website blocking, detentions via facial recognition.

Set up by lawyers from the Agora human rights group, which Russia designated 'undesirable' in 2023.

The only official bot is @nfp_hotline. It never asks for money or SMS codes.

Other ways to reach them: Delta Chat

First Line

Free, anonymous psychological support for people affected by, or afraid of, political persecution and for their families; an information line and safety guides.

Psychological help is in a separate bot, @PsyFirstlineBot: free, anonymous, one-off chat sessions with trained volunteer psychologists, 18+, no diagnoses or medication. Confidentiality is broken if there is a risk of suicide or a threat to a child.

The only official bot is @FirstLineHelpBot. It never asks for money or SMS codes.
