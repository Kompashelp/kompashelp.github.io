Draft What human rights defenders advise

Helping family and prisoners from abroad
  • Help through lawful channels: pay a lawyer under a contract, send letters and parcels. Mediazona knows of no case opened over parcels or paying a lawyer for a relative in pre-trial detention. Transfers are dangerous if the sender understood the money would go to terrorism: in 2024 the Constitutional Court refused to hear the complaint of a woman sentenced to 8 years for transfers to her daughter, who had gone to join IS in Syria, and found the law clear.
  • Rights groups advise helping through organisations: Solidarity Zone, Freedom Zone, Memorial Political Prisoners Support. Check the designation before donating. Since April 2026 Memorial has been designated 'extremist' in Russia. A donation to an 'extremist' organisation can be treated as financing extremism (art. 282.3 CC). This also applies to people living abroad who travel to Russia or pay with a Russian card.
  • Banks can see transfers to relatives. Don't send money to 'banned' organisations or their fundraisers: the risk also falls on the recipient in Russia.
  • In letters to pre-trial detention or a colony, don't write about politics, the case or third parties: censors read everything. You can send a letter by ordinary post or via FSIN-Pismo; the prison service sees the letter and the sender's name.
  • If you are put on the 284-FZ register, dependent relatives can receive a payment from your frozen funds.
  • A lawyer. The law allows you to pay for a relative's lawyer: other people may engage defence counsel with the accused's consent (art. 50 CPC), and a wife or daughter can sign the contract. We found no cases in Russia over paying a lawyer or sending parcels to a relative. Pay the lawyer directly under a contract, not into a frozen account. Don't run a defence fundraiser under the FBK brand.
  • Do not donate to FBK yourself. The US entity of FBK has been 'terrorist' since 12 February 2026; new donations may be treated as financing terrorism (from 8 years). Any such transfer is a risk on a trip: an émigré who came back to visit his mother got 15 years for a 2022 donation to a Ukrainian army fund.
  • Publicity. First Department advises asking the prisoner and the lawyer first: silence is sometimes a defence strategy too. Before visiting family, read 'I want to visit Russia': phones are checked at the border, and relatives of the accused may be called as witnesses.
Safety
  • Do not send money to the account of a relative on the Rosfinmonitoring list: it is frozen. Pay the lawyer directly or help a family member who has their own account.
  • Do not discuss the case with family in messengers. Let the lawyer handle the details.

Who helps

5 organisations · contacts checked against the organisations' sites 26 September 2026

Freedom Zone

Association of political prisoners' relatives: money for families, parcels, paying for lawyers and fines.

Other ways to reach them: zona.svobody@gmail.com

abroad
Get in touch → freedom-zone.com

#free120 campaign

Volunteer campaign for the release of seriously ill political prisoners in Russia. It finds lawyers and doctors for them, pushes the prison service to provide treatment and raises money for families' trips and parcels.

This is mainly an advocacy campaign: a list, letters, publicity. Direct medical help may be limited. No status in Russia found, but the campaign is run by emigrant groups, so write carefully from Russia and do not keep the chat on your phone.

The only official bot is @free120pzkbot, as listed on the campaign's website. Do not write to bots with similar names.

Other ways to reach them: mail@free120pzk.com

abroad
Message the bot → @free120pzkbot

You Are Not Alone (political prisoners support marathon)

Gives money to political prisoners in Russia and their families: for food, medicine, lawyers and trips to visits. Sometimes helps former political prisoners and people who had to flee. Applications are reviewed within 30 working days.

The project itself is not on the lists, but its co-organisers are FBK ('extremist' in Russia) and Meduza ('undesirable'). Applying from Russia carries risk: use a VPN and do not keep confirmations on your phone. In August 2026 some donors' email addresses leaked; the project says applicants' data was not affected.

Other ways to reach them: support@june12.io · application criteria

online
Get in touch → airtable.com

Solidarity Zone

Lawyers and support for people persecuted for anti-war actions; help for political prisoners.

The only official Telegram is the channel @solidarity_zone, as listed on the group's website. It lists no bot; do not write to a bot with the same name.

Other ways to reach them: solidarity_zone@riseup.net
