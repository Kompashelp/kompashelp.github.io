Persecuted in Kazakhstan
  • Cases are most often brought for 'false information', 'inciting discord' and membership of a banned group ('Alga, Kazakhstan!'). Keep copies of your posts and do not delete them without legal advice.
  • Journalists and bloggers: Adil Soz. Activists and human rights defenders: Kazakhstan International Bureau for Human Rights and Front Line Defenders (emergency grants).
  • Lawyers can lose their licence, as Gulnara Bazhkenova's defence counsel did. Use international mechanisms too: the UN Working Group found Marat Zhylanbayev's detention arbitrary.
  • Since 30 Dec 2025 'LGBT propaganda' is banned (fines, up to 10 days' detention); since 2026 the foreign-funding register is wider. Secure your accounts: Access Now can help.

current as of 25 September 2026. More: HRW: World Report 2026, Kazakhstan; IPHR: pressure on media and the 'LGBT propaganda' law

Kazakhstan International Bureau for Human Rights and Rule of Law

Protects Russians in Kazakhstan from extradition and deportation and helps with asylum; also takes cases of Kazakh activists and journalists.

Helps people already in Kazakhstan; lawyer Artur Alkhastov advises Russians. Entering or transiting Kazakhstan is risky: since January 2026 the authorities have extradited and deported political refugees.

Other ways to reach them: Telegram channel @kibhr

Kazakhstan NGO Coalition Against Torture

Helps torture victims in Kazakhstan and their families: lawyers, medical help, a psychologist, Istanbul Protocol reports for court and compensation claims, UN complaints. Free of charge.
