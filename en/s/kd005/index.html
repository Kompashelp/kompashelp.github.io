Psychologists and communities abroad
  • If you are in danger right now or thinking about suicide: 112 and your country's crisis lines. Poland 116 123 and 800 70 2222, Estonia 116 123 (Russian available), Lithuania 112 (Russian available), Israel 1201, USA 988 (Russian interpreter available). In Germany the free TelefonSeelsorge line 0800 111 0 111 works in German. We did not find a separate line for Georgia, so call 112.
  • Anxiety, guilt and burnout after leaving are common. Rights groups advise turning to projects for people who have left: there you don't need to explain the context.
  • Free psychologists. Kovcheg (foreign agent): 4-week online groups via a form on its website. Reforum Help: online and in Berlin, Vilnius, Warsaw, Paris and Tbilisi, including in Belarusian. Reforum is designated 'undesirable' in Russia, and since July 2026 so is Reforum Space in Paris: if you travel to Russia, contact it from a phone other than one that could be seized. In Germany, psychosocial centres for refugees and torture survivors can help (Bundesverband PSZ directory, interpreters available); in Poland, Polskie Forum Migracyjne (in Russian and Belarusian, up to 3 months).
  • Communities: Reforum Space hubs (Berlin, Vilnius, Tallinn, Tbilisi, Budva, Warsaw, Paris) and The Ark's mutual-aid chats by city and profession. Reforum is designated 'undesirable' in Russia. If you plan to travel to Russia, going to its hubs and events may be treated as taking part in an 'undesirable' organisation, so check with a lawyer. The Ark was set up with support from the Anti-War Committee, which is designated 'terrorist' in Russia. In city chats, don't give your full name, address or travel plans: provocateurs turn up in chats.
  • For LGBTQ+ people there is Sphere, and in Germany Quarteera ('undesirable' in Russia). Bear in mind that the 'LGBT movement' is designated 'extremist' in Russia, so write to Sphere from a device that is not checked. In France, Russie-Libertés ('undesirable' in Russia) brings together anti-war Russians. If you travel to Russia, contacting an 'undesirable' organisation or joining its events can lead to prosecution, so check with a lawyer.
  • Check that a chat or bot is official: the link should be on the organisation's website.
  • A psychologist abroad works under the laws of that country. Before the first session, ask what they write down, where they keep it and who they may share it with. For conversations about your case, choose a messenger with end-to-end encryption.

Designations of organisations in this guide: Sphere (foreign agent, "LGBT movement" designated extremist); Reforum Help (undesirable); Quarteera (undesirable); Russie-Libertés (undesirable); Anti-War Committee 'Consuls' (undesirable, terrorist, on Rosfinmonitoring list). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.

Polskie Forum Migracyjne (Polish Migration Forum)

Free short-term psychological support and therapy for up to 3 months in Russian, Belarusian and Ukrainian, crisis support, PTSD assessment. Helps all migrants in Poland.

Book a psychologist by email or by text message to +48 532 474 389 (the number does not take calls).

Other ways to reach them: website

Poland
Get in touch → mailto:psychozapisy@forummigracyjne.org

Psychosocial centres for refugees and torture survivors (Bundesverband PSZ)

A network of about 50 centres across Germany: free therapy and counselling for survivors of torture, war and political persecution, with interpreters. Some centres prepare psychological reports for asylum procedures, for example XENION in Berlin.

Find a centre by German state on the 'Hilfe vor Ort' page. Waiting lists can be long; in Munich booking opens only in certain months.

Other ways to reach them: in English · XENION (Berlin)

Germany
Get in touch → psychosoziale-zentren.org

The Ark (Kovcheg)

Legal advice for people who have left (documents, residence permits, visas, asylum, extradition risk), temporary housing, free psychologists, mutual-aid chats by city.

Lawyers via the Telegram bots @kovcheglive_bot and @EmigrantHelpBot; psychologists and housing via forms on the website. Psychologists: 4-week online groups; waiting time from a few days to weeks. The Ark itself is a 'foreign agent'. The project was set up with support from the Anti-War Committee, designated 'terrorist' in Russia since March 2026. The Kovcheg website footer links to the Anti-War Committee: do not follow that link or subscribe to its channels from a device that could be checked.

Other ways to reach them: team@kovcheg.live

abroad foreign agent
Get in touch → kovcheg.live

Reforum Help

Free psychologists and lawyers for forced emigrants: migration and visas, taxes, civil law, humanitarian residence; groups and seminars. Psychologists work in Russian and Belarusian.

A Reforum project of the Free Russia Foundation, designated 'undesirable' in Russia. If you travel to Russia, contacting an 'undesirable' organisation or joining its events can lead to prosecution, so check with a lawyer. Separately, Reforum Space Paris has also been 'undesirable' since July 2026, and the Reforum Space Vilnius community has been a 'foreign agent' since May 2026. In-person meetings in Paris and Vilnius carry extra risk.

online; Berlin, Vilnius, Warsaw, Paris, Tbilisi undesirable
Get in touch → help.reforum.io

Association of Russian-speaking LGBTQ+ people in Germany: advice for refugees, community and support.

France undesirable

Dapamoga

A Belarusian foundation in Vilnius. It gives free temporary housing, clothes and household items from its warehouse, and helps people settle in Lithuania; it also runs a recovery programme for women who were political prisoners.

Since October 2022 the Belarusian KGB has listed Dapamoga as an 'extremist formation'. People in Belarus have been convicted for 'cooperating' with it: do not contact them from Belarus and do not keep the chat on your phone. Help is mainly for people already in Lithuania.

Sphere

Legal, psychological (up to 4 free sessions with a psychologist) and emergency help for LGBTQ+ people from Russia.

The 'LGBT movement' is designated 'extremist' in Russia. Contact Sphere from a device that is not checked.

The only official bot is @spherehelpbot. It never asks for money or SMS codes.

in Russia and abroad "LGBT movement" designated extremist+ foreign agent
Get in touch ↓

The organisation itself is not designated, but in Russia the 'LGBT movement' is designated 'extremist', and the authorities may present help to LGBT people as taking part in it. Messages on a phone that may be seized are dangerous. Write from a device you would not hand over if detained.
