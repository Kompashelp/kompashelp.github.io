Draft What human rights defenders advise

Persecuted by Azerbaijan, at home or abroad
  • Independent journalists are tried on 'financial' charges such as currency smuggling and tax evasion. In July 2026 Toplum TV staff got 12 to 15 years. Rights groups advise moving archives and source contacts out of reach early.
  • Leaving does not guarantee safety: critics are convicted in absentia, put on Interpol wanted lists and their relatives are pressured.
  • In 2026 Georgia sent journalist Afgan Sadigov back to Baku despite an ECHR interim measure. Rights groups advise avoiding Georgia, even in transit.
  • If you face removal, a lawyer can urgently ask the ECHR for an interim measure (Rule 39). Journalists: CPJ and RSF (both 'undesirable' in Russia); defenders: Front Line Defenders; digital security: Access Now.

Designations of organisations in this guide: Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) (undesirable). Contacting them carries a risk: do not write from a phone that may be seized, and do not repost their materials.

current as of 26 September 2026. More: HRW: Azerbaijan rearrests journalist returned from Georgia; HRW: crackdown on activists in exile; CPJ: Toplum TV sentences

Who helps

5 organisations · contacts checked against the organisations' sites 26 September 2026

Access Now Digital Security Helpline

Free 24/7 help for activists and journalists: hacked accounts, surveillance, securing accounts and devices. Russian-language support available.

Other ways to reach them: help@accessnow.org

worldwide
Get in touch → accessnow.org

Front Line Defenders

Emergency support for human rights defenders at risk: protection grants, secure contact, publicity and advocacy after detention.

For people targeted for peaceful human rights work, including activists and journalists.

Other ways to reach them: protection grants

worldwide
Get in touch → frontlinedefenders.org

Freedom Now

Works to free prisoners of conscience through the UN (Working Group on Arbitrary Detention) and international publicity.

Takes cases selectively; suited to relatives of political prisoners rather than emergencies.

Other ways to reach them: info@freedom-now.org

USA
Get in touch → freedom-now.org

Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ)

Helps journalists at risk: safety advice, emergency assistance, publicity for cases.

Designated 'undesirable' in Russia since May 2026: contacting it from Russia or Russian accounts carries a risk.

Other ways to reach them: emergencies@cpj.org

worldwide undesirable
Get in touch → cpj.org

Reporters Without Borders (RSF)

Assistance to journalists and media: grants, legal support, publicity for persecution.

Designated 'undesirable' in Russia since 2025: if you are a Russian citizen or travel there, factor in the risk.

Other ways to reach them: assistance2@rsf.org

worldwide undesirable
Get in touch → rsf.org
