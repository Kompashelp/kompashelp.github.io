Money, banks and taxes after leaving
  • The EU's 19th sanctions package (October 2025) restricted cards for Russians without a right to reside. Blocks mostly hit people awaiting a residence permit renewal or living on a D visa.
  • According to the European Commission, everyone lawfully in the EU has a right to a basic account; Russian citizenship alone is not a reason to close one. Keep proof of your status to hand.
  • From Russia you can send up to $1m a month to your own foreign accounts, and up to $10,000 via money transfer services. Many Russian banks are cut off from SWIFT.
  • After 183 days abroad in a year you lose Russian tax residence. Remote work under a contract with a Russian company is taxed at 13-22% regardless of status.
  • Since March 2025 'foreign agents' receive Russian income only into a special account. Donations from Russia and via exchanges are traced: Binance handed data to Russia's Investigative Committee.

current as of 25 September 2026. More: Oninvest: Commission guidance for banks; Bank of Russia: transfer restrictions; T-Bank: income tax for non-resident remote workers; First Department: Binance and data for investigators (foreign agent)

The Ark (Kovcheg)

Legal advice for people who have left (documents, residence permits, visas, asylum, extradition risk), temporary housing, free psychologists, mutual-aid chats by city.

Lawyers via the Telegram bots @kovcheglive_bot and @EmigrantHelpBot; psychologists and housing via forms on the website. The project was set up with support from the Anti-War Committee, designated 'terrorist' in Russia in March 2026; The Ark itself is a 'foreign agent'. Psychologists: 4-week online groups via a form on the website; waiting time from a few days to weeks.

Other ways to reach them: team@kovcheg.live

Reforum Help

Free psychologists and lawyers for forced emigrants: migration and visas, taxes, civil law, humanitarian residence; groups and seminars. Psychologists work in Russian and Belarusian.

A Reforum project of the Free Russia Foundation, designated 'undesirable' in Russia. If you travel to Russia, contacting an 'undesirable' organisation or joining its events can lead to prosecution, so check with a lawyer.
