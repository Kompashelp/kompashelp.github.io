Many projects are designated 'foreign agents', 'undesirable' or 'extremist' in Russia, and Belarusian funds 'extremist' in Belarus. Organisations designated 'extremist' in Russia or on the Rosfinmonitoring list only have «Support from abroad» behind a detailed warning; 'undesirable' and Belarusian ones are also behind a warning; 'terrorist' ones have no donation link. What the designations mean →

«Support from abroad»: read before donating

If an organisation is designated 'extremist' in Russia, giving it money is a criminal offence under art. 282.3 of the Russian Criminal Code (financing extremist activity). The law also applies to Russian citizens abroad: a case can be opened in absentia and the person detained on entry. It is relatively safe only for people without Russian citizenship who do not travel to Russia and pay from non-Russian accounts and cards. Do not mention the donation in messages or on social media.

All these projects run on private donations. If you want to help and can, links to their donation pages are below.
