Belarus: the State Control Committee gets access to bank data

Law cards are drafts until a lawyer has reviewed them. This is not legal advice: check the official text via the link.

Signed · in force from 25 January 2027 (main part, including financial investigation bodies' powers against financing 'extremism'; date computed as six months after publication on 24.07.2026) · in force from 1 July 2027 (certain paragraphs of arts. 1 and 2) · in force from 25 July 2026 (other provisions)

172-З of 17 July 2026 «On amending laws on the activities of the State Control Committee bodies»

Who is affected

People and organisations in Belarus whose bank and other data the State Control Committee and financial investigation bodies may request, and people suspected of financing 'extremist' activity (for example, donations).

Who is not affected

The law is mainly about the structure and powers of the State Control Committee; it adds no new penalties for individuals.

What changes

  • The Committee may request from banks information on operations, accounts and deposits of organisations and individuals, including bank secrecy data.
  • Financial investigation bodies are tasked with detecting and stopping the financing of 'extremist' and terrorist activity.
  • Financial investigation bodies are classed as paramilitary organisations and part of the national security forces.

What to do now

  • If you are in Belarus or use Belarusian accounts, assume transfers and donations can be checked.
  • Discuss questions about donations to 'extremist' organisations with a lawyer.

What is not yet clear

The exact in-force dates (24 or 25 July 2026, 24 or 25 January 2027) are not named in the law; we count from publication. How the new powers will be used against individuals is not yet visible.
