Kazakhstan: ban on 'LGBT propaganda'

Law cards are drafts until a lawyer has reviewed them. This is not legal advice: check the official text via the link.

In force · in force from 2 March 2026 (main part, including the ban on 'propaganda of non-traditional sexual orientation'; 60 calendar days after first publication on 31.12.2025)

248-VIII ЗРК of 30 December 2025 «On amendments and additions to certain legislative acts of the Republic of Kazakhstan on archives and restricting the spread of illegal content»

Who is affected

Media, online platforms, advertising, film, schools and users in Kazakhstan who publicly spread information that, in the law's words, 'propagandises' non-traditional orientation or paedophilia.

Who is not affected

The law itself sets no fines or detention for individuals: it amends laws on media, advertising, communications, online platforms, children's rights and others. Any penalty comes from other rules.

What changes

  • It defines 'propaganda of non-traditional sexual orientation': public spreading of information 'to form a positive public opinion'.
  • Such information is banned in media, advertising, on online platforms and in film, and is classed as harmful to children.
  • 13 laws are amended, including on media, advertising, communications and online platforms, which allows access to such content to be restricted.

What to do now

  • If you are in Kazakhstan, keep in mind that public posts and channels on this topic may be blocked.
  • Before publishing or running a community, consult local human rights groups.

What is not yet clear

Neither this law nor the Administrative Code law adopted the same day (247-VIII) contains a separate fine or detention for such 'propaganda'; the source of reports about fines and up to 10 days' detention is not verified.
